雨天中的兩難選擇

從圖片描述、問題辨識，到選擇、行動與考量因素，逐步完成兩段式英文作文。

寫作任務
  • 第一段：描述圖片與學生面臨的困境。
  • 第二段：說明你的選擇，以及做決定時會考量的因素。
  • 全文至少 120 words。

題目圖片與任務解析

先掌握圖片中可確認的資訊，不必自行加入過多背景情節。

核心問題

  1. 學生正趕往一場不能錯過的重要考試。
  2. 老婦人的袋子破裂，物品散落在雨中的地面上。
  3. 學生必須決定：留下幫忙、短暫協助、請他人接手，或以考試為優先。
  4. 第二段的重點是分析決策因素，不是另外敘述一段完整的個人故事。

五種構思版本

選擇一種立場，依照流程發展第一段與第二段。

Sentence Frames

以下句架採通用設計，可依不同立場替換內容。

第一段：描述圖片與困境
Where / When On a rainy afternoon, a student was standing near a bus stop.
Who / Purpose He was on his way to an important examination.
What happened Just then, an elderly woman’s bag broke, causing her belongings to fall onto the ground.
Reaction When he noticed what had happened, he turned around and hesitated.
Problem He had to decide whether to help the woman or hurry to the examination.
Two-sided conflict On the one hand, the examination was about to begin. On the other hand, the woman clearly needed help.
第二段：選擇、行動與考量因素
My choice If I were the student, I would...
Action First, I would... Then, I would...
Factor 1 The first factor I would consider is...
Factor 2 Another important consideration is...
Alternative Rather than handling the whole situation alone, I could...
Conclusion Taking all these factors into account, I believe this would be the most reasonable choice.

Useful Vocabulary and Phrases

依寫作功能選用，不需要全部塞入文章。

場景與事件

  • near a bus stop
  • on a rainy afternoon
  • get off the bus
  • be on one’s way to...
  • belongings fall onto the ground
  • be scattered across the road

兩難與決定

  • face a difficult choice
  • be caught between A and B
  • weigh the options
  • consider the consequences
  • make a reasonable decision
  • find a compromise

協助他人

  • offer help
  • lend someone a hand
  • ask someone nearby for help
  • make sure someone is not left alone
  • ask another person to take over
  • provide brief assistance

考試與時間

  • an important examination
  • be about to begin
  • arrive on time
  • miss the examination
  • have no second chance
  • face a strict time limit

考量因素

  • urgency
  • possible consequences
  • the time required
  • available alternatives
  • other people nearby
  • personal responsibility

連接語

  • just then
  • as a result
  • although
  • on the one hand... on the other hand...
  • rather than
  • taking all these factors into account
